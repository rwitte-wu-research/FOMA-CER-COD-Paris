## 1. K1 gap statement (novelty-bearing; basis of the Ch.-1 first claims)

No meta-analysis exists with CER/environmental constructs as the isolated predictor and the cost of debt as the focal outcome. Nearest neighbors: Zeng et al. (2025) — broad CSR, COD one of two outcomes [HUB, PK]; Bauer, Follert & Stöckl (2025) — systematic review, no pooling [HUB, PK]; Carnevale & Drago (2024) — loan-channel critical review [HUB]. No debt-pricing synthesis tests the Paris Agreement; field-wide exactly one Paris-testing synthesis exists (Zarea et al. 2026 — banking financial performance, subgroup design) [HUB, PK]. The companion meta-analysis (Witte et al. 2026) covers the equity side and leaves the COD question open [PK].

First-claim wordings licensed by this inventory (as frozen in Ch. 1 §a8): (i) first meta-analytic calibration of the CER–COD relationship; (ii) first meta-analytic test of the Paris-repricing thesis **in credit-market pricing**; (iii) first systematic publication-selection assessment **for this literature**.
