## Verdict line (per the resolved gate [DEC-044/DEC-047])

Across all eight pre-registered moderator dimensions the Paris-period contrast shows no detectable
differences: interaction HTZ p-values range from .077 to .799 with a Holm-adjusted minimum of .616
(0/8 significant), no differential pre-trends precede the break (p = .311/.272), and the conditional
Paris coefficient in the unified models is +0.008 (p = .769) and +0.013 (p = .426) — the null
survives conditioning. The pooled association itself stays within a narrow negative band across every
cut (r = −.029 to −.122; the only significant level contrast is loan vs bond, Δz = +.053, p = .009),
consistent with the headline reading: statistically detectable but economically negligible, never
null. Where the post-Paris literature is structurally silent (CDS: none; US: one cluster; fully
cross-classifiable cell: nine clusters, all AsiaPac/developing/non-western/civil-law), the absence is
coding-robust and reported as an evidence-base composition finding; the single near-threshold
quantity of the block (ever-treated coding, p = .058) lies in the pre-registered sensitivity arm and
is carried descriptively in the appendix.
